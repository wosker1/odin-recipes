Hamburger Recipe

Plain burger with cheese

This recipe is for a traditional burger with chesse since we are onto basics

Ingrediets

  • Burger bread
  • New york strips for the meat
  • Basic cheddar chese
  • seasoning basics salt and pepper
  • mayo and something spicy for the sauce

Steps

  1. Grind your meat strips
  2. salt and pepper as your liking
  3. Sear until brown on both sides for about 4 minutes in order to get to a medium well
  4. Add you chesse and let it melt for a minute or two
  5. let the meat rest while you heat the bread
  6. mix the mayo and buffalo sauce for the spicy sauce
  7. add some pickles and enjoy
