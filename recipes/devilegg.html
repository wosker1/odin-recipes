Traditional Deviled Eggs

Traditional deviled eggs, i have never tried them but they seem easy to make

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Mayo
  • Spices like salt, pepper, paprika and red pepper
  • Green onion

Steps

  1. boil the eggs for 8 minutes
  2. one boiled take the yolks into a bowl
  3. add seasoning to your liking from the spices mentioned before
  4. mix with the mayo
  5. fill the whites with the yolk and spices mix
  6. sprinkle some green onions and some chili flakes and enjoy
