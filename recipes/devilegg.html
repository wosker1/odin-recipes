Traditional Deviled Eggs

Traditional deviled eggs, i have never tried them but they seem easy to make

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Mayo
  • Salt, Spice and everything nice
  • Green onions

Steps

  1. boil the eggs
  2. once boiled take the yolks into a bowl
  3. add seasoning salt, spice and everything nice
  4. mix with the mayo
  5. fill the whites with the yolk and spice mix
  6. sprinkle some green onions and some chili flakes and i guess that sould be it idk i have never made them
