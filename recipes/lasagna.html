Lasagna with greens and meat

This lasagna will be the best you ever tried if you are a big fan o extra chesse some greens and fine meats

Ingredients

  • Lasagna layers
  • Parmegiano
  • read meats
  • basil
  • Tomato

Steps

  1. Prepare the meats only with salt and pepper or any other spices as you like
  2. char the tomatoes with butter salt and pepper as you like and blend with some tomatoe paste
  3. once the sauce and the meats are ready prepare to layer your lasagna addign chesse to your liking on eache layer
  4. cook on the oven for 45 mins
  5. add your greens once out of the oven
